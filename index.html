TwoPin

Pin to pin, every km counts.

A simple vehicle logbook for people in Australia and New Zealand who drive for work. Tap GO when you set off, and TwoPin records your kilometres in the background. At tax time, email yourself a professional PDF report.

See how it works

What it does

🚗 Records your trips

Measures kilometres along the road you drive, even with your phone locked or another app open.

📸 Odometer photos

Take a photo of your odometer at the start and end of each trip as proof for your records.

🧾 Tax-ready reports

Daily, weekly, monthly or full financial-year PDF reports, using the ATO cents-per-km or IRD kilometre rates.

☁️ Backed up

Your logbook is saved to your account, so you don't lose it if you change phones.
