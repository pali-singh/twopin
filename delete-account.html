Delete your TwoPin account

Last updated: 12 October 2026

You can delete your TwoPin account and all the data linked to it at any time. There are two ways.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open TwoPin and log in.
  2. Tap Settings at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down to Delete account and tap it.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away, and you're logged out.

Option 2: By email

If you can't use the app (for example, you no longer have your phone), email us at twopin.support@gmail.com:

  • Send it from the email address you use to log in to TwoPin, so we know the request is really from you.
  • Use the subject "Delete my TwoPin account".

We'll delete your account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

All your odometer photosDeleted

Emails you've sent to our support addressKept only as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted

Deleting only some data

You don't have to delete your whole account. In the app you can:

  • Delete a single trip: open History, then tap the trip's delete button.
  • Remove the data stored on your phone: uninstall the app. This doesn't delete your cloud backup. Use one of the options above for that.
Before you delete: deleting can't be undone. You may need your logbook for your tax records, so go to Reports and email yourself a PDF of each financial year first.

Questions? Email twopin.support@gmail.com. Read our privacy policy for more about how we handle your data.
