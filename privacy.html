Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the TwoPin app ("TwoPin", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, how we keep it safe, and how you can see or delete it. TwoPin is a vehicle logbook app for people in Australia and New Zealand.

The short version
  • We only track your location while a trip is running: from when you tap GO until you tap END.
  • We use your information only to run your logbook. We don't sell it, and there are no ads and no advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time, inside the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and password. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, so we can't read them.
  • Your name.
  • Your country (Australia or New Zealand) and vehicle type (petrol, diesel, hybrid or electric). These are used to apply the right kilometre rate.
  • Your ABN (Australia) or IRD number (New Zealand), only if you choose to enter it. It appears on your reports.

Trip information

  • The date, start and end time, duration and distance of each trip.
  • The start and end location of each trip (GPS coordinates and how accurate they were), and the place names or addresses for those points.
  • The purpose you enter for a trip, and whether it was for business or personal use.

Odometer photos

  • Photos you take of your odometer with the in-app camera. TwoPin doesn't access your photo gallery.

Location: how and when

TwoPin uses your phone's precise location only while a trip is running. During a trip, tracking continues in the background, for example with the screen locked or while you use a maps app, so your kilometres are measured correctly. While this happens, Android shows a notification so you always know tracking is on. Tracking stops as soon as you tap END or cancel the trip. TwoPin doesn't collect your location at other times.

During a trip, TwoPin also uses Android's physical activity recognition to tell whether you're driving or have stopped, so it can remind you to end a trip you may have forgotten. This is processed on your phone and isn't stored or sent to us.

Other permissions

  • Display over other apps: shows the small kilometre bubble on top of other apps during a trip. No information is collected through this.
  • Notifications: shows that a trip is running, and sends reminders (for example, to take your end odometer photo).
  • Camera: used only when you choose to take an odometer photo.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, so we can reply.

2. How we use your information

  • To record your trips and build your logbook and reports.
  • To calculate your claim using the ATO or IRD kilometre rates.
  • To back up your logbook and keep it in sync if you change or reinstall your phone.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up codes and password reset codes.
  • To answer your support requests.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't build profiles about you.

We share information only with the service providers that run parts of TwoPin for us, and only as much as they need:

4. Where it's stored and how it's protected

  • Your information is stored on your phone and in our cloud database (Supabase). Our providers' servers may be located outside Australia and New Zealand.
  • Information is encrypted while it travels between your phone and our servers (HTTPS).
  • Database rules mean each account can only access its own trips and photos.
  • Only you can see your logbook. We access it only if you ask us to help with a problem, or if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while you have an account.
  • If you delete a trip, it is removed from your logbook and our database.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your profile, trips and photos straight away. Copies may remain in our providers' encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they're permanently removed.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you, and then deleted.
Tax records tip: the tax office may ask you to keep logbook records for several years. Before deleting your account, email yourself a PDF report so you keep a copy.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: you can view and edit your trips and profile in the app at any time.
  • Delete: delete single trips in History, or your whole account in Settings. See how to delete your account.
  • Turn off features: you can switch off reminders and the bubble in Settings, and withdraw permissions in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Ask us: email us to request a copy of your information or to raise a concern.

If you're not happy with how we handle a privacy concern, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (Australia) or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (New Zealand).

7. Children

TwoPin is for adults who drive for work. It isn't intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and tell you in the app.
